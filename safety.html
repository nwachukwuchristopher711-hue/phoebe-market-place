Stay safe when you buy or sell

Phoebe Market Place connects buyers and sellers. Deals are made directly between you and the other person. We do not hold payments or deliver items yet, so please follow these steps every time.

Before you buy

1. Contact the seller firstAsk questions about the item. Ask for extra photos if you need them.
2. Confirm the priceAgree on the full price, including delivery if any, before you meet.
3. Agree on a meeting pointChoose a busy public place in the daytime, like a market, a shopping centre or near a bank. Tell a friend or family member where you are going, or bring someone along.
4. Check the item properlyMake sure it works and matches the photos and description before you pay.
5. Pay only when the item is in your handDo not pay in advance, and do not send money to someone you have not met.

If you are selling

Be honest in your listingUse your own clear photos and describe any fault. Buyers trust honest sellers.
Check that the money has arrivedOpen your bank app and confirm the money is in your account before you hand over the item. A screenshot of a transfer is not proof.
Meet in a public placeAvoid inviting strangers to your home.

Warning signs

Stop the deal if someone:asks you to pay a "booking fee" or "delivery fee" first, offers a price that is far too good to be true, pushes you to decide quickly, or refuses to meet in a public place.
